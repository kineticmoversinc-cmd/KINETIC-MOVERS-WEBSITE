Markham Neighborhood Spotlight

Where We Move Most Frequently

While we serve every corner of Markham, certain neighborhoods show distinct moving patterns and requirements. Here's a closer look at the areas where our expertise makes the biggest difference.

Unionville

One of Markham's most beloved communities, Unionville features historic Main Street charm, tree-lined streets, and a mix of older homes and newer developments. Moves here often require special consideration for the Main Street heritage district, potential parking restrictions during festivals, and the unique character of areas like the Unionville Greenbelt and the historic Freemason's Hall area.

Cornell

This planned community features modern townhomes, family homes, and extensive green spaces. Moving here requires familiarity with the community's specific bylaws, understanding of the Cornell Community Centre and library access points, and coordination around the extensive trail network and recreational facilities that make Cornell so desirable for families.

Markham Village & Downtown Markham

Markham Village features heritage properties along Main Street Markham, while downtown Markham continues to develop with mixed-use buildings and condo towers. Moving here requires navigating potential heritage restrictions, coordinating with the Markham Village BIA for street access during festivals, and understanding the specific procedures for high-rise condo buildings along Roadhouse Avenue and near the Markham Civic Centre.

FAQ

Markham-Specific Moving Questions

Do I need special permits for moving in Markham?

Most residential moves in Markham don't require special permits. However, for moves involving large trucks on major arteries like Highway 7 or Kennedy Road during peak hours, or for moves to Markham Village that may affect traffic flow during festival season, coordination with the Town of Markham's transportation department may be necessary. Our team handles all necessary permits and coordinates with municipal authorities to ensure compliance.

Are there specific challenges with moving to or from Markham's tech office buildings?

Yes. Markham's technology corridors along Kennedy Road, Enterprise Drive, and Warden Avenue often have specific moving hour restrictions, loading dock requirements, and security clearance needs for certain buildings. We're experienced with the procedures for major tech companies in this area and will advise you on any associated requirements or timing limitations during the planning phase.

How does winter weather affect moving in Markham?

Markham experiences significant snowfall and freezing temperatures from December through March. Our winter-ready fleet includes trucks with snow tires, heated cargo areas for temperature-sensitive items, and crews trained in safe ice and snow navigation. We monitor Markham's specific weather patterns and road conditions closely and will proactively reschedule if conditions become unsafe for transport.

What neighbourhoods in Markham are most challenging for large moving trucks?

Areas with narrow streets or limited turning radius include some older sections of Markham Village, the historic Main Street area, and neighbourhoods with mature tree canopies like those along the Rouge River valley. For these locations, we may recommend a shuttle service using a smaller vehicle to transfer items from the street to your final destination.

How far in advance should I book my Markham move?

For peak moving season (May through September), we recommend booking 4-6 weeks in advance to secure your preferred date. For off-peak months (October through April), 2-3 weeks is usually sufficient. However, for tech office moves requiring specific scheduling or condo moves in downtown Markham, we suggest contacting us as early as possible since building management often requires 2-4 weeks notice for booking.
