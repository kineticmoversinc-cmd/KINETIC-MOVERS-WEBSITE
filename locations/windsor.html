FAQ

Windsor-Specific Moving Questions

Do I need any special permits for my move?

Most residential moves don't require special permits. If your building or street has specific rules—an elevator booking window, a loading dock reservation, a permit for street parking—let us know when you book and we'll plan around it.

Do you move in winter?

Yes. We monitor the weather forecast in the days leading up to your move and will talk through rescheduling with you if road conditions look unsafe for a long-distance drive.

What if my building has narrow streets or limited truck access?

Let us know your address when you book. For locations where a full-size truck can't get close, we may recommend a smaller shuttle vehicle to move items the last stretch.

How far in advance should I book?

For peak season (May through September), we recommend booking 4–6 weeks ahead to secure your date. For the rest of the year, 2–3 weeks is usually enough — though for a long-distance move, earlier is always safer.
