FAQ

Windsor-Specific Moving Questions

Do I need special permits for moving in Windsor border areas?

Most residential moves in Windsor don't require special permits. However, for moves involving large trucks on major border arteries like the Ambassador Bridge approach or Wyandotte Street during peak crossing times, or for moves to or from properties near the border that may affect customs processing, coordination with the City of Windsor or federal border authorities may be necessary. Our team handles all necessary permits and coordinates with municipal authorities to ensure compliance.

Are there specific challenges with moving to or from properties near the Detroit River?

Yes. Properties near the Detroit River often have specific moving hour restrictions due to wind exposure, equipment limitations due to potential wake restrictions, and special handling requirements to protect items from moisture and temperature fluctuations near the waterway. We're experienced with the procedures for riverfront properties and will advise you on any associated requirements or timing limitations during the planning phase.

How does border crossing affect moving in Windsor?

Windsor's position as Canada's busiest border crossing creates unique moving considerations, particularly during peak travel times and shift changes at major plants. Our teams are experienced with moves involving border areas, understanding the specific considerations of roads near customs facilities and potential delay-related considerations. We monitor border wait times closely as major delays can affect scheduling on roads near the border crossings, and we'll proactively reschedule if conditions become unsafe for transport on these unique roadways.

What neighbourhoods in Windsor are most challenging for large moving trucks?

Areas with narrow streets or limited turning radius include some older sections of downtown Windsor, the historic core near Dieppe Gardens, and neighbourhoods with mature tree canopies like those in Riverside and along the riverfront. For these locations, we may recommend a shuttle service using a smaller vehicle to transfer items from the street to your final destination.

How far in advance should I book my Windsor move?

For peak moving season (May through September), we recommend booking 4-6 weeks in advance to secure your preferred date. For off-peak months (October through April), 2-3 weeks is usually sufficient. However, for border area moves or moves requiring specific scheduling near major employers, we suggest contacting us as early as possible since building management or plant authorities often requires 2-4 weeks notice for booking.
