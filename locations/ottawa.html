FAQ

Ottawa-Specific Moving Questions

Do I need special permits for moving in Ottawa government districts?

Most residential moves in Ottawa don't require special permits. However, for moves involving large trucks on major government arteries like Wellington Street or Confederation Boulevard during parliamentary sessions, or for moves to or from properties near government buildings that may affect security protocols, coordination with the City of Ottawa or federal authorities may be necessary. Our team handles all necessary permits and coordinates with municipal authorities to ensure compliance.

Are there specific challenges with moving to or from properties near the Rideau Canal?

Yes. Properties near the Rideau Canal often have specific moving hour restrictions due to heritage considerations, equipment limitations due to potential wake restrictions, and special handling requirements to protect items from moisture and temperature fluctuations near the waterway. We're experienced with the procedures for canal-area properties and will advise you on any associated requirements or timing limitations during the planning phase.

How does government presence affect moving in Ottawa?

Ottawa's high concentration of government offices and embassies creates unique moving considerations, particularly during parliamentary sessions and major international events. Our teams are experienced with moves involving government areas, understanding the specific considerations of roads near secure facilities and potential protocol-related delays. We monitor event calendars closely as major summits or visits can affect security protocols on roads near government corridors, and we'll proactively reschedule if conditions become unsafe for transport on these unique roadways.

What neighbourhoods in Ottawa are most challenging for large moving trucks?

Areas with narrow streets or limited turning radius include some older sections of downtown Ottawa, the historic core near ByWard Market, and neighbourhoods with mature tree canopies like those in the Glebe and along the Ottawa River. For these locations, we may recommend a shuttle service using a smaller vehicle to transfer items from the street to your final destination.

How far in advance should I book my Ottawa move?

For peak moving season (May through September), we recommend booking 4-6 weeks in advance to secure your preferred date. For off-peak months (October through April), 2-3 weeks is usually sufficient. However, for government district moves or moves requiring specific scheduling near parliamentary sessions, we suggest contacting us as early as possible since building management or security authorities often requires 2-4 weeks notice for booking.
