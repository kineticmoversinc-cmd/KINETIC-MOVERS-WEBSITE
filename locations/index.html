Questions about our service area

Is my city not on this list — can you still help?

Possibly. We run regular routes across the GTA and Southwestern Ontario, and handle long-distance moves beyond the cities listed here. Get in touch with your addresses and we'll confirm.

Does pricing change based on which city I'm moving to?

Local moves within the GTA and Southwestern Ontario are quoted the same way regardless of city. Long-distance moves (Ottawa, Windsor, Montreal) factor in travel distance as part of the fixed quote.
