FAQ

Questions about our service area

How much does it cost to hire movers in the GTA?

Every move is priced on its own details — home size, crew, and distance — but the quote is always fixed and usually arrives within one business day. For typical price ranges in our home region, see our Brampton & Peel moving cost breakdown.

How far in advance should I book my move?

Book 4–6 weeks ahead in peak season (May–September); 2–3 weeks is usually enough from October to April. We run daily routes across the GTA from our Brampton base, so same-week moves are often possible — ask us about your date.

Do you handle condo and apartment moves, including elevator bookings?

Yes — condos and apartments are a large share of our work. We coordinate elevator reservations, loading-dock access, and building rules with your property management, from downtown Toronto towers to the Square One corridor in Mississauga and Brampton’s own high-rises.

Is my city not on this list — can you still help?

Possibly. We run regular routes across the GTA and Southwestern Ontario, and handle long-distance moves beyond the cities listed here. Get in touch with your addresses and we’ll confirm.
