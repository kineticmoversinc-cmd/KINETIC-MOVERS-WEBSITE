About Kinetic Movers

Built to keep moving day boring — in a good way

Kinetic Movers Inc. is a Brampton-based moving company serving homes and offices locally across the GTA, with long-distance moving services reaching cities across Canada. We started with a simple idea: a moving quote should be the price you actually pay, and a moving crew should show up when they say they will.

That means fixed quotes based on the details you give us, blanket-wrap protection as standard rather than an upsell, and one point of contact from your first call to the last box off the truck.

At a glance

  • Based at 4 King Cross Rd, Brampton, ON
  • Serving the GTA locally, with long-distance moves across Canada
  • Fully insured residential and commercial crews
  • Residential, packing, and office relocation services
