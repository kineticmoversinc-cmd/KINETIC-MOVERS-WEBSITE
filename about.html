About Kinetic Movers

Meet Kinetic Movers — a licensed, insured moving company in Brampton, Ontario

Kinetic Movers Inc. is a licensed and insured moving company based in Brampton, Ontario, serving homes and offices across the GTA, with long-distance moving routes reaching cities across Canada. We started with a simple idea: a moving quote should be the price you actually pay, and a moving crew should show up when they say they will.

That means fixed quotes based on the details you give us, blanket-wrap protection as standard rather than an upsell, and one point of contact from your first call to the last box off the truck.
