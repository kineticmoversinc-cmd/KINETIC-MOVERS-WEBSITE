FAQ

Common questions

Can you move our office outside of business hours?

Yes — evening and weekend scheduling is standard for commercial moves, specifically to avoid disrupting your operating hours. We plan the timeline with your office manager in advance.

Do you handle IT equipment and servers?

Yes. Computers, monitors, and networking equipment are padded, labelled, and inventoried separately so everything reconnects correctly at the new office. For sensitive server moves, let us know in advance so we can plan accordingly.

Can a large office move be split over multiple days?

Yes. Phased moves are available for larger offices — we can move department by department or floor by floor to keep some operations running during the transition.
