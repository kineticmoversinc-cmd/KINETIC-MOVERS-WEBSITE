Toronto to Windsor Moving FAQs

How much does a Toronto to Windsor move cost?

It depends on your home size, your move date, and any extras like packing or tricky building access. Our GTA-to-Windsor moves are priced with a flat rate based on an accurate inventory — send us your details and we will respond with a fixed quote, usually within one business day. For background on how long-distance pricing works in our region, see our Brampton/Peel moving cost breakdown.

How do flat-rate quotes work?

You share an inventory of what is moving, either through our quote form or a virtual survey. We price the whole Toronto-to-Windsor trip up front — loading, drive time down the 401, and unloading — and lock it in as a fixed quote. The price does not change on moving day, even if traffic runs slow.

What affects the price of my Toronto to Windsor move?

The main factors are the size of your home, how much packing you want us to handle, access at both ends (stairs, elevators, long carries), and your moving date — peak summer dates book up first. Everything is built into your flat-rate quote before you commit.

How long will delivery take?

The drive from the GTA to Windsor is roughly 3.5 to 4 hours down Highway 401. Smaller moves are often delivered the same day; larger ones get a confirmed delivery window at booking, and we keep you posted if traffic shifts it.

Do you run other long-distance routes?

Yes. Alongside the Toronto-to-Windsor corridor, we run regular dedicated routes from the GTA to Ottawa and to Montreal. Ask about return-trip savings if your dates are flexible — pairing your move with a return load can lower the price.
