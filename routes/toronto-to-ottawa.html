Typical GTA–Ottawa Moving Costs

What Does a Toronto to Ottawa Move Cost?

Published route data puts a typical GTA-to-Ottawa move between about $1,660 and $4,742 depending on home size. That's the general industry range — not our price. Your Kinetic quote is always a fixed, flat rate based on your inventory. Here's how the range breaks down.

Studio & 1-Bedroom Moves

Smaller Toronto-to-Ottawa moves sit at the lower end of the published range — fewer items, a smaller truck, and often same-day delivery. Our fixed quote is based on your actual inventory, so you pay for your move, not an average.

2–3 Bedroom Homes

Mid-size family moves land in the middle of the published $1,660–$4,742 range. Add our packing service or an extra stop and your quote adjusts — still fixed, still in writing, still the final price.

4+ Bedroom Homes

Larger homes reach the upper end of the industry range, where a bigger crew and truck make the biggest difference. Because we price the whole trip up front — loading, drive time, unloading — the number in your quote is the number on moving day.

Where the Route Ends

Popular Ottawa Destinations

We deliver across the National Capital Region, but most of our Toronto-to-Ottawa runs end in these areas. Ottawa to Toronto movers run this corridor both ways — ask about return-trip availability.

Downtown Ottawa

One of Ottawa's most distinctive areas, downtown features the historic ByWard Market and a mix of residential and commercial properties near Parliament Hill. Older buildings here sometimes mean tighter stairwells or limited elevator access, so let us know the building type when you book.

Kanata

This western neighbourhood features tree-lined streets and a mix of newer subdivisions, townhomes, and family homes. A straightforward destination for a moving truck, with typical suburban street access.

Orleans

This established eastern neighbourhood features a mix of post-war bungalows, ranch-style homes, and newer developments — a straightforward destination for a moving truck with typical suburban street access.

Toronto to Ottawa Moving FAQs

How much does moving from Toronto to Ottawa cost?

Published route data puts typical GTA-to-Ottawa moves between about $1,660 and $4,742 depending on home size — that is the general industry range, not our price. Kinetic Movers always prices your move with a fixed, flat rate based on your inventory, usually within one business day, so the quote you approve is the price you pay. For more on how pricing works in our region, see our Brampton/Peel moving cost breakdown.

How do flat-rate quotes work?

You share an inventory of what is moving, either through our quote form or a virtual survey. We price the whole Toronto-to-Ottawa trip up front — loading, drive time, and unloading — and lock it in as a fixed quote. There is no hourly meter running, so slow traffic on the 401/417 never raises your bill.

What affects the price of my move?

Home size is the biggest factor, followed by how much packing you want us to handle, access at both ends (stairs, elevators, long carries), and your moving date — summer and end-of-month dates cost more. All of it is built into your flat-rate quote before you commit.

How long does the move take, and when will my things arrive?

The drive from the GTA to Ottawa is about 450 km — roughly 4.5 to 5 hours. Most moves are delivered the same day. We confirm a delivery window when you book and keep you updated on moving day.

Can flexible dates save me money?

Often, yes. When your dates are flexible, we can pair your move with a return trip so our truck doesn't run empty — and we pass the savings to you. We run regular dedicated routes from the GTA to Ottawa, and also to Windsor and Montreal, which gives us more chances to find a match.
