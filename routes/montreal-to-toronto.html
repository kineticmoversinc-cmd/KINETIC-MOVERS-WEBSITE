Montreal Moving Rules

What Makes Montreal Moves Different

Moving out of Montreal comes with a few extras no other Canadian city has. Here's what we plan for on every trip.

July 1 Moving Day

Quebec's famous July 1 moving day — and the whole summer season around it — means extreme demand for movers in Montreal. Book 4–6 weeks ahead for summer dates; our corridor runs fill up fast around Canada Day.

Elevators, Stairwells & Alleys

Many Montreal buildings require elevator reservations and certificates of insurance, and Plateau and Mile End streets can be tight for a full-size truck. Tell us your building details when you request your quote and we'll handle the logistics — and the paperwork.

Packing for a 500 km+ Haul

A 540 km drive means your belongings ride longer, so protection matters more. We blanket-wrap every furniture piece as standard, and our packing service adds proper wrapping for fragile items on long hauls.

Montreal to Toronto Moving FAQs

How much does moving from Montreal to Toronto cost?

It depends on your home size, the 540 km distance, how much packing you need, and building access at both ends. Kinetic Movers prices every Montreal-to-Toronto move with a fixed, flat rate based on your inventory — usually within one business day — so the quote you approve is the price you pay. For background on how pricing works in our region, see our Brampton/Peel moving cost breakdown.

How do flat-rate quotes work?

You share an inventory of what is moving, either through our quote form or a virtual survey. We price the whole Montreal-to-Toronto trip up front — loading, the 5.5 to 6-hour drive, and unloading — and lock it in as a fixed quote. There is no hourly meter running, so traffic or construction never raises your bill.

What affects the price of my move?

Home size is the biggest factor, followed by packing needs, access at both ends (stairs, elevators, long carries), and your moving date — summer and July 1 dates book up fast and cost more. All of it is built into your flat-rate quote before you commit.

How should I pack for a 540 km move?

A 500 km-plus haul means your belongings ride longer, so packing matters more. We blanket-wrap every furniture piece as standard, and our packing service adds double-walled boxes, dish packs, and proper wrapping for fragile items — worth it on a long-distance run. We can pack everything or just the breakables.

Do you deliver outside downtown Toronto?

Yes. We deliver across the Greater Toronto Area, including Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, Oakville and more. See the full list of areas we serve.
