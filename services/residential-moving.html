FAQ

Common questions

How far in advance should I book residential movers?

We recommend booking 2-3 weeks ahead for weekday moves, and 4+ weeks for weekend or end-of-month dates, which fill up fastest. Last-minute requests are sometimes possible — just call and ask.

Do you disassemble and reassemble furniture?

Yes. Beds, sectionals, shelving units, and other furniture that needs to come apart for doorways or stairs are disassembled and reassembled at your new place at no extra charge.

What size truck and crew will I need?

It depends on your home size — typically a 2-person crew with a 16-foot truck for a 1-2 bedroom home, up to a 4+ person crew with a 26-foot truck for a larger detached house. We confirm the right size when you request your quote.
