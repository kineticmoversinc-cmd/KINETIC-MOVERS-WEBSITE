Office & Commercial Relocation

An office move that bleeds into business hours costs you more than the move itself. We schedule commercial relocations around your operating hours — evenings, weekends, or a phased move over a few days — and plan the logistics in advance with your office manager.

Desks, workstations, filing systems, and IT equipment are inventoried before the move so everything lands in the right spot at the new office, not just the right building.

  • Evening and weekend scheduling to protect business hours
  • Workstation and filing system moves with inventory tracking
  • Coordination with building management for freight elevators and loading docks
  • Minimal-downtime planning for small and mid-size offices
