Packing & Unpacking Services

Packing is usually the part people put off, and the part that causes the most damage when it's rushed. Our team packs room by room with proper materials — not whatever boxes were free that week — so fragile items actually survive the trip.

We can pack everything, just the fragile and awkward items, or unpack on the other end so you're not living out of boxes for a month.

  • Full-service or partial packing, your choice
  • Specialty handling for glassware, artwork, and electronics
  • Labelled boxes by room for faster unpacking
  • Unpacking and debris removal available at your new place
