FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to buy my own boxes and packing materials?

No. All packing materials — boxes, tape, packing paper, bubble wrap, and dish barrels for fragile items — are included in your quote, not billed separately per box.

Can you just pack the fragile items and I'll do the rest?

Yes. Partial packing is available if you'd rather handle everyday items yourself and have us take care of glassware, artwork, electronics, and anything else that's easy to damage.

Will you remove the boxes and packing debris after unpacking?

Yes, if you book our unpacking service — we'll unpack, break down the boxes, and haul away the packing debris so you're not left with a pile of cardboard.
