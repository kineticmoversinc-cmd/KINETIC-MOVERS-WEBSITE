FAQ

Common questions

Which service is right for my move?

Most home moves fall under residential moving. If you also need help boxing things up, add packing & unpacking. Office and business relocations fall under commercial moving, which is scheduled around your business hours.

Can I combine multiple services in one booking?

Yes. Packing & unpacking is commonly bundled with a residential or commercial move — let us know when requesting your quote and it's scoped as one job.

Do all services include insurance and blanket-wrap protection?

Yes. Every Kinetic Movers job, regardless of which service, is handled by a fully insured crew with blanket-wrap furniture protection included as standard, not an add-on.
