Why This Checklist Works

GTA-Specific Moving Timeline

Moving in the Greater Toronto Area presents unique challenges - from Toronto's downtown condo regulations to Brampton's growing suburbs and Mississauga's diverse neighborhoods. This timeline accounts for GTA-specific considerations like elevator bookings, parking permits, and seasonal factors.

8 Weeks Before Move

Planning & Preparation

  • Create a moving binder or digital folder for all documents, receipts, and estimates
  • Take inventory of all belongings - consider what to keep, sell, donate, or discard
  • Research moving companies in the GTA - check reviews, licensing, and insurance
  • If moving to/from a condo, request the building's moving policy and elevator reservation procedures
  • Measure large furniture and doorways in both locations to ensure everything will fit
  • Start using up perishable food and cleaning supplies
  • If you have children, research schools in your new area
  • If you have pets, update microchip information and research veterinarians in your new area

6 Weeks Before Move

Booking & Notifications

  • Book your moving company - GTA movers book up quickly, especially for weekends and month-end
  • If moving to/from a condo or apartment, reserve elevators for both buildings (typically requires 2-4 weeks notice)
  • Apply for parking permits if needed - Toronto, Brampton, and Mississauga may require permits for moving trucks on public streets
  • Notify important parties of your upcoming move:
    • Your employer (if relocating for work)
    • Children's schools
    • Doctors, dentists, and veterinarians
    • Banks and credit card companies
    • Subscription services
  • Begin decluttering - host a garage sale, list items online, or donate to local charities
  • Start collecting moving supplies: boxes, packing tape, bubble wrap, and markers

4 Weeks Before Move

Packing & Organization

  • Start packing non-essential items: seasonal clothing, books, décor, and extra linens
  • Pack room by room, labeling each box clearly with contents and destination room
  • Create an inventory list of valuable items for insurance purposes
  • If you have artwork or antiques, consider specialized packing or insurance
  • Use up frozen foods and plan meals around what's left in your pantry
  • Arrange for pet care on moving day if needed
  • If you have children, arrange for childcare on moving day
  • Confirm all utility transfer dates:
    • Electricity (Hydro One, Toronto Hydro, etc.)
    • Gas (Enbridge, etc.)
    • Water (varies by municipality)
    • Internet/cable (Rogers, Bell, etc.)

2 Weeks Before Move

Final Preparations

  • Finish packing all non-essential items, leaving only what you'll need for the last two weeks
  • Defrost and clean your refrigerator and freezer at least 24 hours before moving
  • Confirm moving company details:
    • Arrival time window
    • Number of movers and size of truck
    • Contact information for the crew leader
    • Elevator reservation numbers for both buildings
    • Parking permit information (if applicable)
  • Pack an "essentials box" for each family member:
    • Toiletries (toothbrush, toothpaste, soap, etc.)
    • Medications
    • Change of clothes
    • Basic kitchen supplies (mug, plate, utensils)
    • Important documents (IDs, passports, moving paperwork)
    • Chargers for phones and electronics
    • Snacks and water bottles
  • Clean your current home thoroughly - this makes the final inspection easier and leaves a good impression
  • If you're selling your home, ensure it's staged and ready for final showings

1 Week Before Move

Final Countdown

  • Pack remaining items, leaving only essentials for moving day
  • Confirm all arrangements with your moving company
  • Prepare your appliances for moving:
    • Washers: disconnect hoses and secure the drum
    • Dryers: clean the lint trap
    • Refrigerators: leave doors open to prevent mildew
    • Stoves: clean and remove any loose parts
  • Gather all important documents in one easily accessible place:
    • Moving contract and insurance information
    • Lease agreements or closing documents
    • Birth certificates, passports, and medical records
    • Financial documents and insurance policies
  • Charge all electronic devices and pack chargers in your essentials box
  • If you have plants, make arrangements for their transport or find them new homes
  • Withdraw any cash you might need for tips or unexpected expenses

Moving Day

Execution & Oversight

  • Wake up early and have breakfast - moving day is physically demanding
  • Do a final walkthrough of your home to ensure nothing is left behind
  • Meet the moving crew and review the plan:
    • Point out fragile items and special handling requirements
    • Confirm which items go in the truck first (typically appliances and large furniture)
    • Review the inventory sheet as items are loaded
  • Ensure elevators are reserved and ready for use
  • Keep your essentials box and important documents with you, not in the moving truck
  • If moving with children or pets, ensure they're in a safe, secure location away from the moving activity
  • Stay hydrated and take breaks as needed
  • Do a final check as the truck is being loaded to ensure everything is accounted for

At Your New Home

Settling In

  • Do a walkthrough before unloading to note any existing damage
  • Direct the movers where to place large furniture and boxes
  • Check that elevators are reserved for unloading if needed
  • Have your essentials box readily available
  • Start with essential rooms: bedrooms and bathrooms
  • Assemble beds first so you have a place to rest
  • Unpack the kitchen next so you can prepare meals
  • As boxes are unpacked, break them down for recycling
  • Test all appliances and utilities to ensure they're working properly
  • Check in with the moving crew leader before they leave to address any concerns
  • Take time to explore your new neighborhood:
    • Find the nearest grocery store, pharmacy, and medical clinic
    • Locate schools, parks, and community centers
    • Identify public transportation options

1 Week After Move

Settling & Follow-Up

  • Continue unpacking room by room, focusing on one area at a time
  • Update your address with any remaining organizations:
    • Canada Revenue Agency
    • Ontario Ministry of Transportation (driver's license and vehicle registration)
    • Ontario Health Insurance Plan (OHIP)
    • Insurance providers
    • Professional associations and licensing boards
  • Register your vehicle in Ontario if you've moved from another province
  • Enroll children in new schools if applicable
  • Find new healthcare providers: doctor, dentist, and veterinarian
  • Explore local amenities and services in your new neighborhood
  • Leave a review for your moving company to help others in the GTA
  • If you experienced any issues during the move, follow up with the moving company's customer service
GTA Moving Considerations

Neighborhood-Specific Tips

Toronto Downtown & Core

Moving in downtown Toronto requires special consideration for streetcar routes, bike lanes, and rush hour restrictions. Many buildings require advance notice for moving truck parking, and some have specific moving hour restrictions (often weekdays only, 7 AM - 7 PM).

Brampton & Peel Region

As one of Canada's fastest-growing municipalities, Brampton has diverse moving considerations from historic downtown areas to new developments. Many new condo buildings have specific moving hours and elevator booking requirements that must be coordinated weeks in advance.

Mississauga & West GTA

Mississauga's landscape ranges from the bustling City Centre to waterfront communities like Port Credit. Moving near the waterfront may require additional considerations for moisture protection, and some conservation areas have specific regulations.
