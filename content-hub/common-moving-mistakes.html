Before Moving Day

Planning & Timeline Mistakes

Most moving-day chaos actually starts weeks earlier, with a timeline that was too optimistic from the start.

Starting Too Late

Packing always takes longer than you expect.

  • Underestimating packing time is the single most common mistake
  • Start non-essential rooms (storage, guest rooms) at least 3-4 weeks out
  • Leave only daily-use items for the final week
  • Book your moving company 2-4 weeks ahead, earlier in peak season

Skipping the Declutter Pass

Packing items you'll just donate later wastes time and money.

  • Sort before you pack, not after you unpack at the new place
  • Every box you don't need to pack is time and truck space saved
  • Set aside a donate/sell/discard pile before boxes come out
  • Don't pay to move things you already know you won't keep

Not Getting a Written Quote

Verbal estimates lead to disputes on moving day.

  • Always get a fixed, written quote before booking
  • Confirm what's included: stairs, long carries, appliance handling
  • Ask what could change the price on the day (parking access, extra items)
  • Keep the quote and any confirmation emails until after the move
Packing & Belongings

Mistakes That Damage Your Stuff

Most moving damage isn't the crew's fault - it's how items were packed before the crew ever touched the box.

Overpacking Boxes

A box that's too heavy is a box that fails.

  • Keep book and document boxes small - paper is deceptively heavy
  • Test that you can comfortably lift any box before sealing it
  • Overloaded boxes are more likely to split or blow out the bottom
  • Distribute weight evenly rather than filling one box to the brim

Unlabelled Boxes

Mystery boxes slow down both moving day and unpacking week.

  • Label every box with contents and destination room
  • Mark FRAGILE boxes clearly on multiple sides
  • Number boxes and keep a simple master list for insurance purposes
  • Colour-code by room to help the crew place boxes correctly on arrival

Not Protecting Fragile Items Properly

Bubble wrap alone isn't always enough.

  • Wrap dishes individually and pack them on their edge, not flat
  • Use dish-pack boxes (thicker cardboard) for kitchenware where possible
  • Fill empty space in boxes so items can't shift in transit
  • Photograph valuable or antique items before the move for insurance records
Moving Day & After

Mistakes on the Day Itself

A handful of logistics mistakes account for most of the stress people report after their move.

Forgetting Address Changes

This is the mistake people remember weeks later.

  • Canada Post mail forwarding (set up 1-2 weeks before your move)
  • Driver's licence and vehicle registration address update
  • Bank, insurance, and subscription billing addresses
  • Notify your employer, doctor, and children's school

Not Confirming Building Access

Access issues cause the most day-of delays.

  • Book condo elevators in advance - many buildings require 48+ hours notice
  • Reserve street parking permits ahead of time in dense neighbourhoods
  • Confirm move-in/move-out hours allowed by your building
  • Measure doorways and stairwells for large furniture beforehand

Not Doing a Final Walkthrough

Items and damage are both easy to miss in the rush.

  • Check every closet, cabinet, and cupboard before the truck leaves
  • Check the garage, shed, and any outdoor storage
  • Take photos of the empty unit for your move-out inspection
  • Confirm utilities are off/transferred before locking up
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the single biggest moving mistake?

Underestimating how long packing actually takes. Most people start too late, then end up rushing the final days - which is also when most damage and lost items happen.

How early should I book a mover in the GTA?

2-4 weeks ahead for most moves, earlier if you're moving during peak season (May to September) or need a specific date, since availability books up fast.

What address changes do people forget most often?

Canada Post mail forwarding and driver's licence/vehicle registration updates are the two most commonly missed - both are quick to do online but easy to overlook amid everything else.
