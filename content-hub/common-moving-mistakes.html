GTA-Specific Pitfalls

Moving Mistakes the GTA Does Its Own Way

Toronto-area moves have traps you won't find in smaller towns: high-rise elevator rules, parking permits, and a month-end booking crunch that fills schedules weeks out.

Not Booking the Condo Elevator

In a GTA high-rise, the elevator booking is the move.

  • Most condo buildings require the service elevator booked 2-4 weeks ahead
  • No reservation means the crew waits in the lobby while your booked hours tick away
  • Buildings also want a $2M certificate of insurance from your mover
  • The fix: book the elevator the day you book your mover, then share the confirmation with them

The Month-End Booking Crunch

Half the GTA seems to move on the 1st and the last weekend of the month.

  • Month-end and weekend slots book out weeks ahead — late bookers get leftovers
  • Summer (May-September) weekends disappear fastest
  • Last-minute bookings often mean higher prices or no availability at all
  • The fix: lock in your date 2-4 weeks out (4-6 in summer) — or pick a mid-week date for better rates

Money & Paperwork

Mistakes That Cost You Money

Most moving-day price surprises trace back to one of these three decisions made weeks earlier.

Skipping the Parking Permit

No permit can mean no truck near your door.

  • Dense GTA neighbourhoods often need a temporary on-street parking permit for the moving truck
  • Without one, the crew carries everything further — or the truck gets ticketed mid-move
  • Condo loading docks need reserving too, not just the elevator
  • The fix: check your city's parking rules a week out and have the permit details ready for your movers

Not Getting a Written Quote

Verbal estimates lead to disputes on moving day.

  • Always get a fixed, written quote before booking — we provide one within one business day
  • Confirm what's included: stairs, long carries, appliance handling
  • Ask what could change the price on the day (parking access, extra items)
  • The fix: keep the written quote until after the move, and compare quotes on what's actually included

Underestimating Packing Time

The single most common moving mistake, full stop.

  • Packing always takes longer than you expect — start non-essential rooms 3-4 weeks out
  • Leave only daily-use items for the final week
  • Rushing the last 48 hours is when most damage and lost items happen
  • The fix: work backwards from moving day on a written timeline

Packing & Moving Day

Moving Day Mistakes That Damage or Delay You

The last 48 hours decide whether your move feels smooth or chaotic — these are the mistakes that surface then.

Packing That Breaks Your Stuff

Most moving damage is a packing problem, not a crew problem.

  • Keep boxes liftable — overloaded boxes split or blow out the bottom
  • Wrap dishes individually and pack them on edge, not flat
  • Fill empty space so nothing shifts in transit
  • The fix: label every box with its room and mark FRAGILE on multiple sides

Not Confirming Building Access

Access issues cause the most day-of delays.

  • Re-confirm your condo elevator booking the week of the move — most buildings need 2-4 weeks' notice to book
  • Have street parking permits and loading-dock reservations ready
  • Confirm move-in/move-out hours allowed by your building
  • The fix: send your movers the building contact and your reservation details ahead of time

Skipping the Walkthrough & Address Updates

The mistakes you discover after the truck leaves.

  • Check every closet, cabinet, garage, and storage locker before the truck leaves
  • Set up Canada Post mail forwarding 1-2 weeks before the move
  • Update your driver's licence, vehicle registration, bank, and insurance
  • The fix: run both the walkthrough and the address-change list the night before
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the single biggest moving mistake?

Underestimating how long packing actually takes. Most people start too late, then end up rushing the final days - which is also when most damage and lost items happen.

How early should I book a mover in the GTA?

2-4 weeks ahead for most moves, earlier if you're moving during peak season (May to September) or need a specific date, since availability books up fast.

What address changes do people forget most often?

Canada Post mail forwarding and driver's licence/vehicle registration updates are the two most commonly missed - both are quick to do online but easy to overlook amid everything else.

Are uninsured movers really that risky?

Yes. If an uninsured crew damages your building's hallway or drops a heavy item, the claim comes to you - and most GTA condos won't let them through the door without a $2M certificate of insurance. A fully insured mover like Kinetic Movers Inc. protects you, your building, and your belongings.
