Moving During Different Seasons: A GTA Guide

Each season in the GTA presents unique moving challenges and opportunities. From navigating winter snow and ice to managing summer heat and humidity, our seasonal moving guide provides specific tips for moving safely and efficiently regardless of the time of year.

December to February

Winter Moving Considerations

Winter moves are quieter and often cheaper - but ice, snow, and short daylight hours need real planning.

Weather & Safety

The biggest winter risk isn't cold, it's slips.

  • Salt or clear walkways and driveways before the crew arrives
  • Lay down floor protection for slush and melting snow tracked indoors
  • Keep a weather backup date in mind for severe storm days
  • Warm up the truck and vehicles well before loading starts

Protecting Your Belongings

Cold and moisture can damage certain items in transit.

  • Electronics need time to adjust to temperature before powering on
  • Wooden furniture can crack in extreme cold - wrap and pad extra carefully
  • Plants generally shouldn't travel in an unheated truck
  • Liquids (cleaning supplies, paint) can freeze and crack containers

The Upside of a Winter Move

There's a reason movers who plan around it save money.

  • Winter is the GTA's lowest-demand season, with more date flexibility
  • Often better rates than peak summer season
  • Easier to book on shorter notice
  • Less competition for elevator/parking bookings in condo buildings
June to August

Summer Moving Challenges

Summer is the GTA's busiest moving season - which brings its own set of things to plan around.

Heat & Hydration

Long moving days in summer heat are harder on people than boxes.

  • Keep water available for the crew and your family throughout the day
  • Schedule an early morning start to avoid the hottest part of the day
  • Take breaks in shade or air conditioning when possible
  • Watch for signs of heat exhaustion, especially on multi-hour moves

Protecting Heat-Sensitive Items

Some belongings genuinely don't handle a hot truck well.

  • Electronics, vinyl records, and candles can warp or melt in a hot truck
  • Keep medications and anything temperature-sensitive with you, not in the truck
  • Wine and certain foods should travel in a vehicle, not a cargo hold
  • Musical instruments are especially sensitive to heat and humidity swings

Booking in Peak Season

Summer is when the GTA moving market is tightest.

  • Book 4-6 weeks ahead if possible - summer weekends fill up fast
  • Expect higher rates than off-peak months
  • Mid-week moves still offer better availability and pricing than weekends
  • Confirm your date early, especially around the popular July 1st period
March-May & September-November

Spring & Fall Moving Tips

Shoulder-season moves in the GTA offer a good balance of moderate weather and reasonable pricing - with a few things still worth planning for.

Unpredictable Weather

Spring and fall in Ontario can swing widely day to day.

  • Check the forecast in the days leading up to your move
  • Have rain protection ready for furniture and boxes just in case
  • Spring thaw can mean muddy entryways - lay down floor protection
  • Fall moves should account for shorter daylight hours later in the season

Ideal Timing Windows

These seasons often hit the sweet spot for GTA movers.

  • Moderate temperatures are easier on people, furniture, and electronics
  • Less demand than summer means more schedule flexibility
  • Good visibility and road conditions for the moving truck
  • Popular with families avoiding a mid-school-year move

Seasonal Housing Market Timing

Spring and fall are also active real estate seasons.

  • Spring is peak home-buying season in the GTA - book movers early
  • Fall often sees a secondary market surge before winter
  • Closing dates can shift - keep your mover in the loop on any changes
  • Have a flexible plan if your closing date moves by a few days
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the cheapest season to move in the GTA?

Winter (December through February) is typically the lowest-cost and most flexible season, since it's the slowest time of year for moving companies.

Is it harder to book a move in the summer?

Yes - summer, especially July and August, is peak moving season in the GTA. Booking 4-6 weeks ahead is a good idea if your dates are flexible enough to plan that far out.

Do I need to do anything special for a winter move?

Mainly safety prep: clear and salt walkways before the crew arrives, protect floors from snow and slush, and let electronics adjust to indoor temperature before plugging them in.
